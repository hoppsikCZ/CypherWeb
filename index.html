Encode or decode a message with a classic cipher.

About these ciphers

Caesar

Shifts each letter by a chosen number of places in the alphabet. With a shift of 3, HELLO becomes KHOOR. Decoding shifts the letters back.

ROT13

A Caesar cipher with a fixed shift of 13. Applying ROT13 twice returns the original text, so the same operation encodes and decodes.

Atbash

Replaces each letter with its opposite in the alphabet: A becomes Z, B becomes Y, and so on. Applying it again reverses the transformation.

These classic ciphers are useful for learning, but they do not provide real security.
